## AI Call Center

The rates below are for the **AI Call Center** — agents that answer the phone, act in your systems, and hand over to a person. The other five products are priced further down.

| What you are billed for | Unit | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A live call with an agent | Connected minute, rounded up | **$0.09** |
| An agent turn over the API | Turn, plus the speech it speaks | **$0.02** |
| Speech — Sada, the flagship voice tier | 1,000 characters | **$0.05** |
| Speech — Nabra, the high-volume tier | 1,000 characters | **$0.02** |
| Transcription | Minute, rounded up | **$0.03** |
| Drafting an agent from your website | Agent | **$0.06** |
| Rewriting a prompt into Arabic | Prompt | **$0.02** |

A live call is charged by the minute rather than by turn, because that is how a line is actually consumed: it is held open whether or not anyone is speaking, and a caller thinking in silence costs what a caller talking costs.

### What a minute includes

Everything between the caller and the answer. Hearing them in Najdi, Gulf, Egyptian or Modern Standard Arabic, working out what they want, calling into your systems to do it, speaking the reply, and writing the bilingual transcript and summary afterwards.

There is nothing metered on top of the minute for dialect coverage, for actions, or for transcripts.

### What a month looks like

At $0.09 a minute, an hour of agent conversation costs $5.40.

| Calls per month | Average length | Agent minutes | Cloud cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | 3 minutes | 1,500 | **$135** |
| 2,000 | 4 minutes | 8,000 | **$720** |
| 10,000 | 4 minutes | 40,000 | **$3,600** |
| 50,000 | 5 minutes | 250,000 | **$22,500** |

Size the deployment from peak concurrency rather than from the monthly total — the busiest hour of the busiest day is what has to survive. See [Concurrency](/telephony#concurrency).

<Tip>
  Two levers move this number more than the rate does: how fast the agent gets to the point, and how many calls it resolves without a handover. A minute saved on average call length is worth more than a discount at most volumes.
</Tip>

### Building and testing

Building an agent costs almost nothing. A test call in the console is charged at the live rate and capped at five minutes, so a tab left open cannot quietly spend the balance. Previewing what an agent says is charged as speech, at the tier the agent uses.

Capacity is committed and prepaid for the year. The more you commit, the lower the rate — and running it on your own GPUs takes 30% off, because that is real infrastructure you are providing.

| Committed hours per year | On Voho-provided GPUs | On your own GPUs |

## Choosing between cloud and on-premise

On-premise engagements start at **$150,000 a year** across licence and capacity. At cloud rates that is roughly 1.6 million agent minutes — far more conversation than most operations have. Below that floor the managed cloud service is the better commercial fit and the honest recommendation.

So take on-premise when an obligation genuinely requires processing to stay inside your environment, not to reduce the bill. If the driver is a general security preference rather than a specific rule, the managed service on an enterprise agreement is the better trade. See [Data residency](/on-prem/residency) for what each configuration actually commits to.

## Paying

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Prepaid credit" icon="credit-card">
    Cloud usage draws down a balance you top up in advance, from $5 to $5,000 a payment. Auto-recharge tops it up from a saved card when it falls below a threshold you set, under a fixed daily ceiling that cannot be raised from the console.

    When the balance reaches zero, the API returns `402 insufficient_credit` rather than running up a bill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invoicing" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Enterprise, private cloud and on-premise agreements are invoiced rather than charged to a card. Licence and committed capacity are prepaid annually in advance — that prepayment is what makes the committed rates possible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Currency and VAT" icon="percent">
    Rates are quoted in US dollars and exclude VAT. Where Voho is registered to collect it, VAT is added at checkout or on the invoice. Card statements read `VOHO AI* CREDIT`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If you exceed your commitment" icon="chart-line">
    Overage is billed quarterly in arrears at your tier rate. If your usage crosses into a better tier, that rate applies from the point you cross it rather than at renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If you under-use" icon="hourglass">
    Committed hours do not roll into the next year. Commit to the volume you are confident of and top up mid-year rather than over-committing at signature — a top-up at your tier rate costs the same as having committed it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
