## How it compares

Most voice AI is quoted as a platform fee with the expensive parts left out — you then pay separately for the language model, the speech recognition, the voice and the telephony. This is what a minute costs once those are added.

| | All-in, per minute |
| --- | --- |
| **Voho** | **SAR 0.15 · \$0.04** |
| Flat-rate international providers | SAR 0.34 · \$0.09 |
| International platforms, once models are added | SAR 0.41 – 1.13 · \$0.11 – \$0.30 |
| Regional managed packages | SAR 7,500 – 15,000 a month, minutes not published |

No provider in the Saudi market publishes a per-minute price. This one is published, and it is the price.

## What is never included

**Telephony.** Renting the number and carrying the calls are the phone company's charges, quoted by region and passed through at cost with nothing added — see [Phone numbers](/numbers). If you already have a trunk or a PBX, Voho connects to it and there is nothing further to buy.

**Setting it up**, if you want that done for you rather than by your own team: a one-off **SAR 37,500 / \$10,000** for the first workflow on any deployment, and **SAR 18,750 / \$5,000** for each one after it.
