Joins the systems a facilities team runs between — Archibus, SAP, BMS, ticketing — so a reported fault becomes a work order, an assignment and a closed loop without anyone retyping it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SAR 0.15 per work order" icon="building">
    A fault resolved into an asset, a trade, a priority and an SLA.
  </Card>
  <Card title="\$0.04 per work order" icon="dollar-sign">
    The same price at the peg of SAR 3.75 = \$1.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If tenants report faults by **phone**, the call is priced as [AI Call Center](/pricing/ai-call-center) — SAR 0.26 / \$0.07 a minute — and the work order it produces is included in that. The per-work-order rate is for faults arriving by app, form or API.
</Note>

## Not included

**Writing the ticket into Archibus or SAP.** Voho produces the work order; posting it into your system of record is an integration, connected once. A one-off SAR 37,500 / \$10,000 if you want it done for you. See [Integrations](/integrations).
