Reads the paperwork a process is waiting on — invoices, contracts, IDs, delivery notes, handwritten forms and bad scans — and returns the fields the next system needs, in Arabic and English.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SAR 0.04 per document" icon="file-invoice">

  </Card>
  <Card title="\$0.01 per document" icon="dollar-sign">

  </Card>

## Where it runs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cloud">
    **SAR 0.04 / \$0.01 a document.** No monthly minimum and no page bundles to buy in advance.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="In-Kingdom hosted">
    **Quoted against your deployment**, not sold from a price list — a dedicated deployment operated for you and sized to your concurrency.

    Where processing physically happens is the entire point of this tier, so it is settled during scoping and written into the contract rather than asserted on a pricing page. If in-country processing is a requirement you have to evidence to a regulator, **On-premise** answers it without qualification.

## What a month costs on the cloud

| Documents a month | Riyals | Dollars |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 | **SAR 188** | \$50 |
| 50,000 | **SAR 1,875** | \$500 |
| 250,000 | **SAR 9,375** | \$2,500 |

Riyal totals are at the peg; the per-document figure above is rounded up to the nearest halala.

## Not included

**Writing the result into the system that was waiting for it** — your ERP, your ledger, your case management. Connecting the first one is a one-off SAR 37,500 / \$10,000 if you want it done for you. See [Integrations](/integrations).
