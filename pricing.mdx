## One rate

That one number covers the whole call: understanding Najdi, Gulf, Egyptian or Modern Standard Arabic, working out what the caller wants, doing it in your systems, speaking the reply, and writing the transcript and summary afterwards.

There is no seat fee, no platform fee, no per-agent charge, no charge for dialects, and no charge for transcripts.

<Tip>
  Committing to 50,000 minutes a year brings it to **SAR 0.11 / $0.03** a minute.
</Tip>

## How that compares

Most voice AI is quoted as a platform fee with the expensive parts left out — you then pay separately for the language model, the speech recognition, the voice, and the telephony. The number below is what a minute actually costs once those are added.

| | All-in, per minute |
| --- | --- |
| **Voho** | **SAR 0.15 · $0.04** |
| Flat-rate international providers | SAR 0.34 · $0.09 |
| International platforms, once models are added | SAR 0.41 – 1.13 · $0.11 – $0.30 |
| Regional managed packages | SAR 7,500 – 15,000 a month, minutes not published |

No provider in the Saudi market publishes a per-minute price. This one is published, and it is the price.

## Where it runs

The price of the conversation does not change with the deployment. What changes is what sits underneath it.

| | Riyals | Dollars |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cloud** — managed by Voho, nothing to install | SAR 0.15 a minute | $0.04 a minute |
| **In-Kingdom hosted** — a dedicated deployment in a Saudi data centre, data stays in the Kingdom | SAR 0.15 a minute<br />+ SAR 7,500 a month | $0.04 a minute<br />+ $2,000 a month |
| **On-premise** — your servers, your GPUs, your building | SAR 112,500 a year<br />**minutes not metered** | $30,000 a year |
| **Air-gapped or multi-site** — nothing reaches the internet | SAR 281,250 a year<br />**minutes not metered** | $75,000 a year |

On-premise is a flat annual licence and nothing per minute. You are already paying for the hardware, so the only limit on how much you use it is how much your own GPUs can carry. See [Requirements](/on-prem/requirements) for what a node handles.

<Note>
  Setting the first workflow up against your systems is a one-off **SAR 37,500 / $10,000**, on any deployment, and only if you want it done for you rather than by your own team. Each further workflow is SAR 18,750 / $5,000.
</Note>

## What is not included

**Telephony.** Renting the number and carrying the calls are the phone company's charges. They are quoted by region and number type and passed through at cost with nothing added — see [Phone numbers](/numbers). If you already have a trunk or a PBX, Voho connects to it and there is nothing further to buy.

That is the whole list. Your CRM and ticketing licences stay yours, and on an on-premise deployment so do the GPUs.

## Paying

Cloud usage runs from a prepaid balance you top up by card, from SAR 19 / $5 upwards, and stops rather than overruns when it reaches zero. Anything with a committed rate, a hosted deployment or an on-premise licence is invoiced instead, annually in advance.
