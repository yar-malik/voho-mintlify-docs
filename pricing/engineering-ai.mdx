Answers questions about the drawings, manuals, standards and maintenance history your engineering team already has, and cites the document and page it took the answer from.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SAR 0.15 per question" icon="drafting-compass">
    Priced per question asked, not per document indexed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="\$0.04 per question" icon="dollar-sign">
    The same price at the peg of SAR 3.75 = \$1.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Indexing your archive costs nothing. You are charged when somebody asks it something, including when the honest answer is that the archive does not say.

## Where it runs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cloud">
    **SAR 0.15 / \$0.04 a question.** No per-engineer seat and no charge for the size of the archive.

    Your archive and the answers drawn from it are stored in Saudi Arabia.

    **White-label is included**. Your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="In-Kingdom hosted">
    **SAR 0.15 / \$0.04 a question, the same as the cloud.** No premium and no monthly platform fee.

    Your archive and the answers drawn from it are stored in Saudi Arabia.

    Dedicated in-Kingdom model processing, on GPUs deployed and operated by Voho inside the Kingdom, is available on an enterprise agreement.

    **White-label is included**. Your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="On-premise">
    **Included in the platform licence: SAR 112,500 / \$30,000 a year, nothing metered.**

    Usually the right answer here. Engineering archives tend to be the drawings a company will not send anywhere, and an offline site cannot call out even if policy allowed it.

    **White-label is included**. Your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Not included

**Indexing an archive that is not already digital.** Scanning is not something Voho does. If the drawings are on paper, that is a separate exercise and worth scoping before anything else.
