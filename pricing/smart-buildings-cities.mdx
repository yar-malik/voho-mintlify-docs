Joins the systems a facilities team runs between — Archibus, SAP, BMS, ticketing — so a reported fault becomes a work order, an assignment and a closed loop without anyone retyping it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SAR 0.04 per work order" icon="building">
    A fault resolved into an asset, a trade, a priority and an SLA.
  </Card>
  <Card title="\$0.01 per work order" icon="dollar-sign">
    The same price at the peg of SAR 3.75 = \$1.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If tenants report faults by **phone**, the call is priced as [AI Call Center](/pricing/ai-call-center) — SAR 0.26 / \$0.07 a minute — and the work order it produces is included in that. The per-work-order rate is for faults arriving by app, form or API.
</Note>

## Where it runs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cloud">
    **SAR 0.04 / \$0.01 a work order.** No per-building licence and no charge for the size of the asset register.

    Reported faults and the work orders they become are stored in Saudi Arabia. Model processing runs on Voho's global infrastructure — see [Where your data lives](#where-your-data-lives).

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="In-Kingdom hosted">
    **Quoted, on an enterprise agreement.** Models run on dedicated GPUs inside Saudi Arabia, deployed and operated by Voho, so processing stays in the Kingdom as well as storage.

    Take this tier when a regulator or a contract requires in-country **processing** that you have to evidence. It is priced against your concurrency rather than per minute, because dedicated hardware is dedicated whether or not it is busy.

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="On-premise">
    **Included in the platform licence — SAR 112,500 / \$30,000 a year, nothing metered.**

    Worth it where the BMS itself is on an isolated network, which is common.

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Where your data lives

| | Storage | Model processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cloud, pay-as-you-go and API** | Saudi Arabia | Voho's global infrastructure |
| **In-Kingdom, on enterprise** | Saudi Arabia | Saudi Arabia, on dedicated GPUs |
| **On-premise** | Your own environment | Your own environment |

Reported faults and the work orders they become are stored in Saudi Arabia on every plan, pay-as-you-go and API included.

If your obligation covers where processing happens and not only where data rests, that is an enterprise or [on-premise](/on-prem/overview) deployment — both run the models on GPUs inside the Kingdom.

## Not included

**Writing the ticket into Archibus or SAP.** Voho produces the work order; posting it into your system of record is an integration, connected once. A one-off SAR 37,500 / \$10,000 if you want it done for you. See [Integrations](/integrations).
