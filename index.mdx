Voho voice agents answer your phone lines, hold a real conversation in Saudi Arabic or English, take the action the caller asked for, and leave your team a clean written record of what happened.

They are built for local operations first. Agents understand Najdi and other Gulf accents rather than treating Arabic as one flat language, switch between Arabic and English mid-call the way callers actually do, and follow up on WhatsApp when a conversation needs to continue after the call ends.

## What an agent does on a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answers">
    The agent picks up, greets the caller in your configured dialect, and starts listening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understands">
    Speech is transcribed and interpreted in real time, including code-switching between Arabic and English.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acts">
    The agent classifies the request and calls your systems — logging a ticket, checking an order, adjusting a setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reports">
    You get a transcript and a structured summary in both Arabic and English, plus any records the agent created.
  </Step>
</Steps>
