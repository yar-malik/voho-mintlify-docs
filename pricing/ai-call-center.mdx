Agents that answer your lines in Saudi Arabic, do what the caller asked in your systems, and hand over to a person when they should.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SAR 0.15 per minute" icon="phone">
    Every minute an agent spends on a call. Nothing else is metered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="\$0.04 per minute" icon="dollar-sign">
    The same price at the peg of SAR 3.75 = \$1.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

That one number covers the whole call: understanding Najdi, Gulf, Egyptian or Modern Standard Arabic, working out what the caller wants, doing it in your systems, speaking the reply, and writing the transcript and summary afterwards.

No seat fee, no platform fee, no per-agent charge, nothing extra for dialects, nothing extra for transcripts.

## Where it runs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cloud">
    **SAR 0.15 / \$0.04 a minute.** Managed by Voho — nothing to install, live in days.

    No minimum, no commitment, no annual contract. You pay for the minutes you actually take, and you can stop.

    Recordings, transcripts and call records are stored in Saudi Arabia. Model processing runs on Voho's global infrastructure — see [Where your data lives](#where-your-data-lives).

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="In-Kingdom hosted">
    **Quoted, on an enterprise agreement.** Models run on dedicated GPUs inside Saudi Arabia, deployed and operated by Voho, so processing stays in the Kingdom as well as storage.

    Take this tier when a regulator or a contract requires in-country **processing** that you have to evidence. It is priced against your concurrency rather than per minute, because dedicated hardware is dedicated whether or not it is busy.

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="On-premise">
    **SAR 112,500 / \$30,000 a year. Minutes are not metered.**

    Voho on your own servers and GPUs. However many calls you take, the licence is the price — your hardware is the only limit. See [Requirements](/on-prem/requirements) for what one node carries.

    Air-gapped or multi-site is **SAR 281,250 / \$75,000 a year**.

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Where your data lives

| | Storage | Model processing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cloud, pay-as-you-go and API** | Saudi Arabia | Voho's global infrastructure |
| **In-Kingdom, on enterprise** | Saudi Arabia | Saudi Arabia, on dedicated GPUs |
| **On-premise** | Your own environment | Your own environment |

Recordings, transcripts and call records are stored in Saudi Arabia on every plan, pay-as-you-go and API included.

If your obligation covers where processing happens and not only where data rests, that is an enterprise or [on-premise](/on-prem/overview) deployment — both run the models on GPUs inside the Kingdom.

## What a month costs

| Calls a month | Average call | Minutes | Riyals | Dollars |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | 3 minutes | 1,500 | **SAR 225** | \$60 |
| 2,000 | 4 minutes | 8,000 | **SAR 1,200** | \$320 |
| 10,000 | 4 minutes | 40,000 | **SAR 6,000** | \$1,600 |
| 50,000 | 5 minutes | 250,000 | **SAR 37,500** | \$10,000 |

<Tip>
  A receptionist in Saudi Arabia costs SAR 2,000 to 5,000 a month and works eight hours a day, five days a week. The first row is a line answered every hour of every day for less than a tenth of one salary.
</Tip>

Past roughly 750,000 minutes a year, the on-premise licence is cheaper than the cloud as well as more private.

## Not included

**Telephony** — the number and the call carriage are the phone company's charges, passed through at cost. See [Phone numbers](/numbers).

**Setting up the first workflow**, if you want it done for you: a one-off SAR 37,500 / \$10,000.
