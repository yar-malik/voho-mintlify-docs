This one is not really a product you meter. It is the deployment underneath the other five: models running where your compliance team has approved them, with your data staying inside that boundary.

<Note>
  If you are here because of a residency requirement, the number you want is the **On-premise** tab below. The per-check rate only applies to the boundary check run against the managed cloud.
</Note>

## Where it runs

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On-premise">
    **SAR 112,500 / \$30,000 a year.** Your servers, your GPUs, your building. Nothing is metered — not calls, not documents, not questions — because you are already paying for the hardware.

    One licence covers whichever of the six products you run on it.

    **SAR 281,250 / \$75,000 a year** for air-gapped or multi-site: nothing reaches the internet, licensing is checked against a local signed file, and the line keeps answering with no route out.

    See [Data residency](/on-prem/residency) and [Requirements](/on-prem/requirements).

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="In-Kingdom hosted">
    **SAR 0.15 / \$0.04 a check — the same as the cloud.** No premium and no monthly platform fee.

    The records checked and the reports on them are stored in Saudi Arabia.

    Dedicated in-Kingdom model processing, on GPUs deployed and operated by Voho inside the Kingdom, is available on an enterprise agreement.

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Cloud">
    **SAR 0.15 / \$0.04 a check.** The boundary check on its own: paste a record and see exactly what would be stripped before it left your estate.

    Useful for showing a compliance team what the control does. It is not a residency answer in itself — on your own deployment the same check runs inside the boundary and nothing leaves at all.

    The records checked and the reports on them are stored in Saudi Arabia.

    **White-label is included** — your brand on the console your clients sign into, and on the reporting they receive, at no additional charge. See [White-label](/partners/white-label).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What that replaces

An on-premise licence is flat and annual, so the cost of running more through it is zero. Past roughly 750,000 call minutes a year it is cheaper than the cloud as well as more private.

| | Year one | Year two |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform licence | SAR 112,500 · \$30,000 | SAR 112,500 · \$30,000 |
| First workflow set up for you | SAR 37,500 · \$10,000 | — |
| **Total** | **SAR 150,000 · \$40,000** | **SAR 112,500 · \$30,000** |

Hardware is not included and stays yours. See [Requirements](/on-prem/requirements) for what a node needs to be.

## Not included

**The GPUs**, and **telephony** if you are running the call centre on it.
