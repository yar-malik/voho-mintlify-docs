Agencies, systems integrators, and telecom resellers deploy Voho on behalf of their own clients rather than for a single organisation. The platform is built for that: each client gets their own workspace, and you keep an administrative view across all of them.

<Note>
  This is a partner arrangement, agreed per agency. Terms depend on volume, the number of client accounts, the deployment model, and who holds first-line support — see [Commercial model](#commercial-model).
</Note>

## How it is structured

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A workspace per client" icon="users-rectangle">
    Each client's agents, numbers, transcripts, and users live in their own workspace. One client never sees another's calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agency administration" icon="user-gear">
    Your team works across every client workspace from one place — creating accounts, configuring agents, and stepping in when something needs fixing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage per client" icon="chart-line">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Your pricing, not ours" icon="tag">

## Onboarding a client

The path is the same one you already run for any other service you resell.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workspace" icon="folder-plus">
    A new client account, isolated from your other clients, with your team holding administrative access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the agent" icon="headset">
    Dialect, voice, instructions, and the actions that reach into that client's systems. See [Writing instructions](/instructions) and [Actions](/actions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the line" icon="phone">
    A number provisioned for the client, or their existing trunk pointed at the agent. See [Telephony](/telephony).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before it answers real calls" icon="vial">
    Run the client's awkward cases — heavy dialect, code-switching, interruptions — before the line is live. See [Testing](/testing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over reporting" icon="chart-pie">
    Decide what the client sees themselves and what arrives as a report from you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many clients and agents

There is no published per-account cap on client workspaces or agents. What a deployment supports in practice is decided by capacity — concurrent calls at peak — and agreed in your partner terms rather than fixed in advance.

Size it from the calls, not the account count. Forty clients with one quiet line each is a smaller deployment than one national contact centre.

## Commercial model

Partner terms are agreed per agency and depend on:

- **Volume** — expected call minutes across all your clients.
- **Client count** — how many accounts in the first year, and the growth shape.
- **Deployment** — managed cloud, in-country hosted, or [on-premise](/on-prem/overview).
- **Support** — whether you hold first-line support for your clients, or Voho does.
- **Billing** — whether Voho bills your clients directly or bills you and you bill them.

## What to bring to a partner conversation

1. Expected daily and monthly call volume across your clients.
2. Average call length.
3. Number of clients in the first six to twelve months.
4. Agents per client.
5. Inbound, outbound, or mixed.
6. New numbers or existing ones — see [Phone numbers](/numbers).
7. Preferred deployment model.
