Every line needs a number in front of it. You can take one from Voho, or keep the numbers you already publish and point them at an agent.

## Bringing numbers you already have

The lowest-friction option, and the right one if your number is already printed on invoices, vehicles, or a website.

Nothing about the number changes. Your carrier keeps terminating it, and your phone system routes the call to the agent over SIP like any other destination. See [Telephony](/telephony). No porting, no notice period, no window where callers reach a dead line.

## New numbers in the region

Voho arranges provisioning across Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf, including +966 numbers.

What is available depends on three things, and they interact:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Number type" icon="list-check">
    Geographic, national, toll-free, and mobile numbers have different rules and different inventory. A toll-free number and a local geographic number are not interchangeable requests.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Direction" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Some numbers are provisioned for inbound only. If you intend to run outbound campaigns from a number, say so at the start. It changes what can be issued and what the carrier requires.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Carrier inventory" icon="warehouse">
    Availability is real-world inventory in the country in question, not a catalogue that is always in stock. Lead times vary by number type and by market.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Bring your requirement as a sentence rather than a quantity: "inbound geographic numbers in Riyadh for three client sites, outbound from one national number." That is enough to check availability. "Ten Saudi numbers" is not.
</Note>

## Verification and documentation

Number provisioning in Saudi Arabia is subject to the requirements of the terminating carrier and to applicable Saudi regulation. This is a carrier process, not a Voho one, and Voho cannot shortcut it.

The exact requirements are confirmed against your case: your company structure, the intended use, the number type, and whether the numbers are inbound, outbound, or both. Expect to identify the legal entity taking the numbers and the person authorised to sign for it.

<Warning>
  Do not commit a launch date to your own customers before the number requirement is confirmed with the carrier. Verification is the step that most often moves a go-live date, and it is outside Voho's control.
</Warning>

Numbers and call carriage are quoted separately from the platform, by region and number type, and passed through at cost. Voho does not mark up carrier minutes.
