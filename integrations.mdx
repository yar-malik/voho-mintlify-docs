An agent that cannot reach your systems is a very expensive answering machine. There are two directions to wire up, and most deployments need both.

## During the call

Actions are the integration surface that matters most. Each one calls a single endpoint in one of your systems, authenticated as a service account you control, and returns something the agent can say out loud.

Design them for speech and for latency, a three-second lookup is three seconds of silence on the line. See [Actions](/actions) and [Latency](/latency).

## Webhooks

Voho can notify your systems when a call ends, so a completed conversation triggers whatever comes next: a CRM record, a ticket, a notification to a team channel, a follow-up task.

This is what makes an agent part of an operation rather than a silo. The alternative, someone reading transcripts and re-keying them, undoes most of the value.

## Transcripts and exports

Every call leaves a transcript and a bilingual summary. Those are available in the console for people, and through the API for systems that need to store, search, or report on them.

Common destinations: a data warehouse for quality reporting, a CRM record attached to the customer, or an archive that satisfies a retention obligation. See [Transcripts and summaries](/transcripts) for what a record contains, and [Security and data handling](/security) for what to settle about retention first.

## Automation tools

Tools like n8n, Zapier, and Make connect through the same REST API and webhooks, no dedicated connector required.

The typical shape is a loop: a webhook from Voho starts a workflow, the workflow updates a CRM or creates a ticket, and a step in that workflow can trigger an outbound call back to the customer.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Trigger a workflow from a call" icon="bolt">
    A finished call fires a webhook into your automation tool, which routes on the outcome: a complaint opens a ticket, a qualified lead lands in the CRM, an emergency pages someone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trigger a call from a workflow" icon="phone-arrow-up-right">
    Your workflow calls the Voho API to place an outbound call: a reminder the day before an appointment, a callback after a form submission. See [Telephony](/telephony#inbound-and-outbound).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep systems in sync" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Push what the agent collected into the system of record, so the next person who talks to that customer sees it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
