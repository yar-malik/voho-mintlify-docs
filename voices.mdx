Every synthesis request names a voice through the `voice` field. Voices differ in dialect, timbre, apparent age, and delivery style — the choice sets the character of the output more than any other parameter.

```json
{
  "text": "أهلاً بك في فوهو",
  "voice": "layla",
  "model": "sada-1"
}
```

<Tip>
  Fetch the catalog programmatically with [`GET /v1/voices`](/api-reference/voices) so your application stays current as voices are added.
</Tip>

## Arabic voices

The core of the catalog. Najdi leads because it is the accent this product is built around.

### Najdi — Saudi Arabia

| Voice | Gender | Age | Styles | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `layla` | Female | 18–30 | Professional, Formal | Warm Najdi delivery. The default for inbound reception and appointment setting. |
| `nouf` | Female | 30–50 | Professional | Measured and senior. Suits collections, escalations, and compliance scripts. |
| `faisal` | Male | 30–50 | Professional, Formal | Even, authoritative Najdi. Reads long policy text without fatiguing the listener. |
| `omar` | Male | 18–30 | Casual, Energetic | Bright and quick. Built for outbound offers and short confirmations. |

### Gulf, MSA, and Egyptian

| Voice | Gender | Age | Locale | Styles | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reem` | Female | 18–30 | `ar-AE` | Casual, Energetic | Gulf accent with a light, conversational lift. |
| `khalid` | Male | 60+ | `ar` | Formal | Modern Standard Arabic, broadcast register. Announcements and IVR trees. |
| `yousef` | Male | 30–50 | `ar` | Professional, Formal | Neutral MSA. The safest choice when the caller dialect is unknown. |
| `maha` | Female | 30–50 | `ar-EG` | Casual, Professional | Egyptian dialect, unhurried and reassuring. |

<Note>
  When you do not know which dialect your callers speak, `yousef` or `khalid` in Modern Standard Arabic is the safe default. MSA reads as formal but is understood everywhere; a mismatched regional dialect sounds foreign.
</Note>

## Dialects

| Dialect | Filter value | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Najdi | `najdi` | Central Saudi Arabia — the default for Saudi deployments |
| Gulf | `gulf` | UAE and wider Gulf |
| Modern Standard | `msa` | Formal, pan-Arab, understood everywhere |
| Egyptian | `egyptian` | Egypt and widely understood across the region |
| Hijazi | `hijazi` | Western Saudi Arabia |

Filter the catalog by any of these:

```bash
curl "https://app.voho.ai/v1/voices?dialect=najdi&gender=female" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $VOHO_API_KEY"
```

## Choosing a voice

Service and support lines read better with an unhurried delivery — `layla` or `nouf` in Arabic, `clementine` or `vespera` in English. Formal and official lines want `faisal` or `khalid`. Sales and outbound can carry the pace of `omar`, `astra`, or `cupola`.

<Warning>
  Test with callers who speak the way your customers actually speak, not colleagues reading a script. Accent coverage is the most common source of early failures, and it shows up within a handful of real calls.
</Warning>

## Auditioning quickly

[`GET /v1/voices/{id}`](/api-reference/voices#get-a-voice) returns a ready-to-send `sample_request` in the right language for that voice — post it straight to `/v1/speech` to hear the voice without writing anything.

```bash
curl https://app.voho.ai/v1/voices/layla \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $VOHO_API_KEY" | jq .sample_request
```
