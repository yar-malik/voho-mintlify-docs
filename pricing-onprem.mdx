<Note>
  Indicative list pricing for **voice agents deployed on-premise**. Every on-premise deployment is quoted against your actual concurrency, workflows and environment — treat these as the shape of a deal and the range to plan a budget against, not a final quote.

  The Speech API and the managed cloud service are priced separately.
</Note>

## How it is priced

Three components, plus telephony. The split matters: you pay for the platform once a year, and for the conversation your callers actually have.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Platform licence" icon="key">
    Annual, by deployment size. The agent runtime, dialect models, actions, console, updates and support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Voice capacity" icon="clock">
    Prepaid annually in hours of conversation. Discounted when you bring your own GPUs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Operations" icon="gauge-high">
    Optional. Voho runs the deployment, or your team does and it costs nothing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Voice capacity

Capacity is committed and prepaid for the year. The more you commit, the lower the rate — and running it on your own GPUs takes 30% off, because that is real infrastructure you are providing.

| Committed hours per year | On Voho-provided GPUs | On your own GPUs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20,000 | $4.50 / hour | $3.15 / hour |
| 75,000 | $3.60 / hour | $2.50 / hour |
| 200,000+ | $2.90 / hour | $2.00 / hour |

<Tip>
  20,000 hours a year is roughly 55 hours of conversation a day. Size it from peak concurrency and average call length — see [Requirements](/on-prem/requirements) — rather than from a headcount figure.
</Tip>

An hour is an hour of agent conversation, measured on connected call time. Silence while the caller waits for a person after handover is not billed.

## Platform licence

| | Annual | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Single node** | $85,000 | One GPU node. A production line, one site. |
| **Cluster** | $180,000 | Up to four nodes, multi-workflow, SSO, SCIM and SIEM streaming. |

## Implementation

A guided implementation with a named solutions engineer, sized to the first workflow.

| | One-off |
| --- | --- |
| Implementation, including the first workflow live | from $60,000 |
| Each additional workflow | $15,000 |

Covers environment review, install, telephony integration, the first workflow built against your systems, testing on real traffic and handover to your operators. See [Getting started](/on-prem/quickstart).

## Telephony

Quoted separately by region and passed through at cost. Voho does not mark up carrier minutes.

## Commercial terms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Minimum commitment" icon="scale-balanced">
    On-premise engagements start at **$150,000 per year** across licence and capacity. Below that, the managed cloud service is the better commercial fit and the honest recommendation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment" icon="credit-card">
    Capacity and licence are prepaid annually in advance. This is what makes the committed rates possible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If you exceed your commitment" icon="chart-line">
    Overage is billed quarterly in arrears at your tier rate. If your usage crosses into a better tier, that rate applies from the point you cross it rather than at renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If you under-use" icon="hourglass">
    Committed hours do not roll into the next year. Commit to the volume you are confident of and top up mid-year rather than over-committing at signature — a top-up at your tier rate costs the same as having committed it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is not included" icon="circle-minus">
    Hardware, telephony carriage, and the systems the agent acts in. GPUs are yours to provide unless you take the Voho-provided option.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
