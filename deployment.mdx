Voho runs in two configurations. Most customers start on the managed service and stay there. Organisations with regulatory or contractual constraints on where processing happens deploy on-premise instead.

## Managed cloud

The default. Voho operates the infrastructure, you connect a phone number and configure your agents. There is nothing to install and nothing to maintain, and new capabilities reach you without a deployment on your side.

This suits most teams, including regulated ones — the questions worth asking are about data handling and retention rather than about who owns the hardware.

## On-premise

On-premise deployment runs Voho inside infrastructure you control, so call audio and transcripts are processed in your environment rather than ours. Organisations choose it for data-residency obligations, security policies that prohibit external processing of customer conversations, or contractual terms with their own clients.

On-premise is scoped per engagement rather than self-served. What gets deployed depends on which capabilities you need, your expected call volume, and the hardware you have available.

It can run in your own data centre on your own GPUs, in your private cloud, in a Saudi data centre, or fully disconnected from the internet.

<Warning>
  On-premise is offered through a scoping engagement, not as a self-service install. Contact Voho before planning a rollout so requirements can be sized against your actual environment.
</Warning>

### What a scoping conversation covers

- **Capabilities** — which parts of the platform run locally, and whether anything remains connected.
- **Capacity** — expected concurrent calls, which determines hardware.
- **Environment** — what you have available, and what needs provisioning.
- **Operations** — who runs it, how it is monitored, and how updates are applied.
- **Data handling** — what is retained, where, and for how long.

### Choosing between them

Take on-premise when an obligation genuinely requires processing to stay inside your environment. It moves real operational responsibility to your team — capacity planning, monitoring, and updates all become yours.

If the driver is general security preference rather than a specific obligation, the managed service with the right contractual terms is usually the better trade.

## Scale

Both configurations are sized against peak concurrent calls rather than monthly volume — the busiest hour of your busiest day is what the deployment has to survive. Bring that figure, your average call length, and the inbound/outbound split to scoping. See [Concurrency](/telephony#concurrency).

## Getting calls in

Neither configuration requires you to replace your phone system. Voho connects over SIP to the trunk or PBX you already run, or provisions numbers for you.
