Your clients should experience the service as yours. White-label covers the surfaces they actually touch: the portal they sign into, the reports they receive, and the person they contact when something is wrong.

It is included on every deployment — managed cloud, in-Kingdom hosted and on-premise — at no additional charge.

## What stays shared

Some things are the platform, not the skin on it. Being straight with clients about this avoids a bad conversation later:

- **Voice and dialect behaviour** is the same platform capability across every partner — see [Voices](/voices).
- **Capacity** is drawn from the deployment you and Voho sized together, and one client's peak affects the pool your other clients draw from.
- **Release timing** follows the platform, not each client's change window, on the managed service. An [on-premise](/on-prem/overview) deployment gives you control of that.

## Isolation between clients

Each client workspace is separate: agents, numbers, call records, transcripts, and users. One client cannot see another's calls, and a user belongs to the workspace you put them in.

Your agency team is the exception by design — administrative access spans your clients so you can operate the estate. Decide deliberately who on your team holds it.

<Warning>
  You are handling your clients' customer conversations, which makes you a processor in your own right. Your contracts with your clients need to say what is retained, for how long, and where it is processed — and those answers have to match what you have agreed with Voho. See [Security and data handling](/security).
</Warning>

## Before you put your name on it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agree the branding scope" icon="palette">
    Which surfaces carry your brand, in writing, before it is in a pitch deck.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle data handling" icon="shield-check">
    Retention, processing location, and what happens at contract end — for your clients, not just for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the support path" icon="headset">
    What your client can raise with you, what you escalate, and the response times each side commits to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the billing model" icon="credit-card">
    Whether Voho bills you or your clients, and how usage is reconciled against what you invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>
