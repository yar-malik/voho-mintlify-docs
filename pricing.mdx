## The short version

A call is **SAR 0.15 / $0.04 a minute**, and that covers the whole call. Everything that is not a call is a cent a run. On your own hardware it is a flat annual licence and nothing is metered at all.

## Pick the deployment

The price of the work does not change with the deployment. What changes is what sits underneath it.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cloud">
    Managed by Voho. Nothing to install, live in days, no minimum and no commitment. You pay the per-minute or per-run rate and nothing else.

    Right for everyone unless a written obligation says otherwise.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="In-Kingdom hosted">
    A dedicated deployment in a Saudi data centre, operated for you. Data stays in the Kingdom, and you still do not run anything.

    The same per-minute rate, plus **SAR 7,500 / $2,000 a month** for the dedicated deployment.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="On-premise">
    Your servers, your GPUs, your building. Call audio and records never leave your environment.

    A flat **SAR 112,500 / $30,000 a year**, and **nothing is metered** — you already pay for the hardware, so the only limit is what your own GPUs carry. Air-gapped or multi-site is **SAR 281,250 / $75,000 a year**.

    See [Data residency](/on-prem/residency) and [Requirements](/on-prem/requirements).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How it compares

Most voice AI is quoted as a platform fee with the expensive parts left out — you then pay separately for the language model, the speech recognition, the voice and the telephony. This is what a minute costs once those are added.

| | All-in, per minute |
| --- | --- |
| **Voho** | **SAR 0.15 · $0.04** |
| Flat-rate international providers | SAR 0.34 · $0.09 |
| International platforms, once models are added | SAR 0.41 – 1.13 · $0.11 – $0.30 |
| Regional managed packages | SAR 7,500 – 15,000 a month, minutes not published |

No provider in the Saudi market publishes a per-minute price. This one is published, and it is the price.

## What is never included

**Telephony.** Renting the number and carrying the calls are the phone company's charges, quoted by region and passed through at cost with nothing added — see [Phone numbers](/numbers). If you already have a trunk or a PBX, Voho connects to it and there is nothing further to buy.

**Setting it up**, if you want that done for you rather than by your own team: a one-off **SAR 37,500 / $10,000** for the first workflow on any deployment, and **SAR 18,750 / $5,000** for each one after it.
