## What a minute includes

A minute is a minute of agent conversation on a connected call. Recognition, the agent's reasoning, synthesis, actions against your systems, and the transcript and summary afterwards are all part of it.

Voice minutes are passed through at cost — Voho does not mark up the speech minutes themselves.

## What is quoted separately

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Telephony and numbers" icon="phone">
    Number rental and call carriage are carrier costs, quoted by region and number type and passed through at cost. See [Phone numbers](/numbers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Implementation" icon="wrench">
    Building the first workflow against your systems, where you want that done for you rather than by your own team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The systems the agent acts in" icon="plug">
    Your CRM, ticketing, and scheduling licences remain yours. See [Integrations](/integrations).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What enterprise adds

- **Reserved capacity** against your peak concurrency, rather than best effort.
- **A service level**, with the response commitments written down.
- **Residency options**, including in-country hosted deployment — see [Deployment options](/deployment).
- **SSO and SCIM**, so access follows your directory.
- **Audit-log streaming** into your own SIEM.
- **Security documentation** for procurement review.
- **A named solutions team** rather than a shared queue.
